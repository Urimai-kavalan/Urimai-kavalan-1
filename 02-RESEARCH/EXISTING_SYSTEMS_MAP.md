## Landscape Analysis Findings

*(Source: PRD Section 2 — Problem Statement; Section 35)*

The August 2026 landscape analysis found:

1. **ICJS/e-Prisons** — internal government infrastructure; not family-facing
2. **e-Courts/NJDG** — public case-status portals; not action-oriented for families
3. **Research systems** (IBPS/NyayaRAG/NyayaAI) — academic/research; not citizen-facing civic-tech
4. **Nyaya-Sahaya** — closest student-built analogue; does not cover the full scope

**Conclusion from landscape:** No identified platform combines the family-facing, voice-first, statute-grounded and human-routed scope proposed for Urimai Kaavalan.

## Research Gaps (Phase 0)

The following areas require further research in later phases and are **not resolved** in Phase 0:

- Formal ICJS API documentation and access process
- DLSA district-by-district legal-aid SOP variations
- State-specific Prison Act provisions
- NALSA-approved PLV qualification standards
- e-Courts public API terms of service
- Tamil Nadu / pilot state specific legal-aid data

## Important Note on Research Boundaries

All research documented here is **sourced from the PRD**. Phase 0 does not involve:
