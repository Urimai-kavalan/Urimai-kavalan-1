## Key Research Findings (from PRD)

*(Source: PRD Sections 2, 35)*

1. **ICJS/e-Prisons** are internal government infrastructure — not accessible to families
2. **e-Courts/NJDG** are public portals — not action-oriented or vernacular
3. **Research systems** (IBPS/NyayaRAG/NyayaAI) are academic — not civic-tech
4. **Nyaya-Sahaya** is the closest student-built analogue — does not cover the full scope
5. **No identified platform** combines family-facing + voice-first + statute-grounded + human-routed
