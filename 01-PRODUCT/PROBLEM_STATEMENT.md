# Urimai Kaavalan — Problem Statement

> **Source:** 03-PRD/01_URIMAI_KAAVALAN_GOVERNMENT_PRD.pdf (Section 2 — Problem Statement)
> **Phase:** 0 — Governance & Research

---

## Problem Statement

*(Source: PRD Section 2, verbatim)*

1. **Families may struggle** to understand case status, legal terminology, documents, deadlines and available legal-aid pathways.

2. **Existing justice infrastructure is fragmented** from the perspective of a family member who needs a simple, vernacular, actionable interface.

3. **Legal information may exist but not be translated** into plain-language next steps.

4. **Potential statutory-review signals can be missed** when no one notices or raises the relevant issue.

5. **Voice/feature-phone access and low-literacy support** are central accessibility requirements for the intended user journey.

---

## Context

*(Source: PRD Section 2, research basis)*

The August 2026 analysis identified:
- **ICJS/e-Prisons** as internal government infrastructure
- **e-Courts/NJDG** as public case-status portals
- **Research systems** such as IBPS/NyayaRAG/NyayaAI
- **Nyaya-Sahaya** as the closest student-built analogue

The analysis concludes that **no identified platform** combines the family-facing, voice-first, statute-grounded and human-routed scope proposed for Urimai Kaavalan.

---

## What Is Being Solved

Urimai Kaavalan addresses the **access gap** — not the judicial decision gap.

The problem is not that courts are wrong. The problem is that:
- Families cannot easily access or understand information about their family member's case
- Legal-aid pathways are not surfaced in an accessible, vernacular format
- Potential statutory review signals may go unnoticed without a structured detection layer
- There is no integrated, human-verified routing layer connecting families to authorized legal-aid

---

## What Is NOT Being Solved

- Urimai Kaavalan does not fix the justice system's structural delays
- Urimai Kaavalan does not predict bail outcomes
- Urimai Kaavalan does not replace qualified legal representation
- Urimai Kaavalan does not make government databases publicly accessible beyond what is already public
