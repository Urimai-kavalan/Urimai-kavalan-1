# Urimai Kaavalan — Product Vision

> **Source:** 03-PRD/01_URIMAI_KAAVALAN_GOVERNMENT_PRD.pdf (Sections 1–4, 36)
> **Note:** The source PRD was originally titled "NYAY-SATHI". This project has been renamed "Urimai Kaavalan". All requirements are preserved from the source PRD.

---

## Vision Statement

Urimai Kaavalan is an AI-assisted legal-access and undertrial-support platform for families of undertrial prisoners in India.

It is designed as an **access, intelligence and coordination layer** around India's existing justice and legal-aid ecosystem.

---

## Core Principle

> **AI assists. Verified law explains. Humans verify. Courts decide.**

---

## Tagline

**Protecting Legal Rights Through AI-Assisted Access**

---

## What Urimai Kaavalan Is

*(Source: PRD Section 1 — Executive Summary; Section 3 — Product Positioning)*

- A **digital bridge** between families, legal-aid institutions and India's justice ecosystem
- An **assistive layer** for access, understanding, triage, routing, monitoring and accountability
- A platform providing **family-facing access**, voice/vernacular support, statute-grounded legal information, eligibility screening, human legal review and routing into authorized legal-aid support

---

## What Urimai Kaavalan Is NOT

*(Source: PRD Section 3 — Product Positioning)*

- **Not an AI judge**
- **Not an autonomous bail-decision system**
- **Not a replacement for lawyers**
- **Not a replacement for ICJS/e-Prisons/e-Courts**
- **Not a replacement for NALSA/SLSA/DLSA processes, police systems, prisons or courts**
- **Not a parallel data silo** to government justice infrastructure

---

## Platform Objectives

*(Source: PRD Section 4 — Objectives)*

1. Reduce friction between families and legitimate legal-aid pathways
2. Make case information understandable in simple language and vernacular formats
3. Surface potential legal-review signals with transparent evidence and citations
4. Connect eligible/priority cases to authorized legal-aid professionals
5. Provide proactive, trackable milestones and alerts
6. Create an auditable human-in-the-loop workflow
7. Enable government users to monitor queues, SLAs and unresolved access gaps
8. Prepare the platform for standards-based integration rather than parallel data silos

---

## Final Product Principle

*(Source: PRD Section 36)*

> Urimai Kaavalan should be built as a trustworthy civic-tech system where AI detects, verified law explains, humans verify, and the justice system decides. The project's credibility comes from interoperability, legal-source governance, human control, accessibility, security and measurable operational improvement — not from claiming that an AI can predict or decide court outcomes.
